02 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

A curious mind.
A deliberate eye.

From a memorable logo to a compelling presentation, I care about how design makes people feel.

I’m a graphic and multimedia designer based in Doha, with experience across Qatar and Morocco. My work connects brand identity, digital content, and presentation design — always with a strong visual idea at its core.

At Yahala Events, I create graphics, multimedia content, and presentations for brand activations, automotive launches, and exhibitions. My focus is graphic and multimedia design. I do not offer 3D modeling or rendering.
